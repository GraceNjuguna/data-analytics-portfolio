Car Sales and One-Year Value Retention Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyses car sales data across different manufacturers and models, comparing vehicle values at the point of sale with their values one year later. The analysis focuses on Japanese manufacturers: Toyota, Subaru, Nissan, Mitsubishi and Honda.

The objective was to identify which manufacturers and models retained the most value after one year, examine sales performance within Toyota's market segment, and explore the relationship between vehicle power and units sold.

Key Findings

Best value retention: Toyota recorded the highest average value retention among the Japanese manufacturers analysed.

Lowest value retention: Mitsubishi experienced the greatest average loss in vehicle value over the one-year period.

Top Toyota model: The Toyota Celica had the highest value retention within the Toyota segment, retaining an average of approximately 92% of its value, equivalent to a 13% decrease in value.

Toyota sales performance: The Toyota Camry recorded the highest number of units sold within the Toyota segment, followed by the Toyota Corolla. Grey was the leading colour reported for Camry sales.

Vehicle power and sales: The analysis did not establish a conclusive relationship between vehicle power and units sold, so further investigation would be needed to determine whether a meaningful association exists.

Tools and Analytical Approach

Tools: Microsoft Excel and IBM Cognos Analytics.

Data analysis: Comparison of vehicle values at sale and after one year, manufacturer-level analysis, model-level value retention, and sales performance analysis.

Data visualisation: Dashboards presenting key performance indicators, comparisons and trends to support data-driven insights.

Business Value

The findings provide insights into vehicle depreciation, value retention and sales performance. Such analysis can help inform vehicle purchasing decisions, resale value assessments and further market research.
